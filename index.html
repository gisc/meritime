A cultural clock, not a medical instrument

In traditional Chinese medicine, 子午流注 associates each two-hour period with a meridian. This clock illustrates that tradition. It does not measure organ activity or establish that an organ works only at these hours. It is not a diagnosis, treatment or health recommendation.

本时钟展示传统中医子午流注学说，不用于诊断、治疗或提供健康建议。经络时段不代表器官只在该时段工作。
